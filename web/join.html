Join Us in Developing VoteTorrent

VoteTorrent is a work in progress, and we need your help to bring it to fruition. We invite developers, testers, and anyone interested in secure and fair elections to join our community. By contributing your skills and feedback, you can help shape the future of voting.

Here are some ways you can get involved:

  • Coding: Contribute to the development of VoteTorrent by writing and reviewing code. Check out our GitHub repository for issues and feature requests.
  • Testing: Help us test VoteTorrent to ensure it is secure, reliable, and user-friendly. Your feedback is crucial in identifying and fixing bugs. Preview builds for Android are available below.
  • Providing Feedback: Share your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve VoteTorrent. Your input helps us make the system better for everyone.
  • Promoting the Project: Spread the word about VoteTorrent to your community and network. The more people involved, the stronger and more effective the system will be.

Visit our GitHub Repository to get started. Reach out there or by email to let us know how you can support the development of VoteTorrent.

Together, we can create a secure and trustworthy voting system for the future.

Try the Preview Builds

These are work-in-progress builds, not finished apps. They are meant for developers and testers who want to follow along and report problems — not for conducting a real election. Expect rough edges, incomplete features, and changes that may wipe out data between builds.

There are two apps. Most people want the Voter app; the Authority app is for the officials and organizations who set up an election.

Voter App

For participating in elections: register, cast your ballot, and verify for yourself that your vote was counted correctly in the totals.

Download Voter APK

Android only · Build date and release notes

Authority App

For election administrators: create an authority, define an election, invite officials, and manage the events that voters participate in.

Installing on Android

Download the APK on your phone and open it. Android will warn you that the file came from outside the Play Store and ask you to allow installation from this source — that is expected for a pre-release build. These apps are not yet in the Play Store or App Store, and they will not update themselves, so check back here for newer builds.

iOS builds are not yet available for outside testing.

Run into something broken? That is exactly what we want to hear about — open an issue on GitHub or email us.
