Public Election View

Prototype of apps/VoteTorrentPublic — the no-login page a visitor opens from a shared link. Tokens, class names, copy, the fact model, the phase derivation and the roll's disclosure and district fold are transcribed from source.

Most fact cards render a label and nothing else. That is not an omission in this prototype — it is the shipped state: in facts.js ten of the eighteen facts carry sentenceKey: null. Only the voter roll, the keyholder key release and the six gap cards have bodies today.

The phase is derived, never picked: each state hands the page an election timeline and an instant, and derivePhase (transcribed from election-phase.js over timeline-core.js) walks closed → settling → voting newest-first. The bar above the page shows the derived phase, its finest stage and how many timeline diagnostics the parse raised — diagnostics never reach the page.

The voter roll does not collapse — there is nothing to click before it shows. Its disclosure is the column policy in roll-disclosure.js, and the same policy gates the district chart: withhold District and the chart is gone too.

The district bars are hand-drawn SVG standing in for Recharts, built to the same contract: horizontal bars, single hue, 140px name band, a count label past the bar end, whole-number ticks only, 4 ticks below 400px of chart width and 6 above. Hover a bar for its tooltip.

No theme toggle: the app ships one dark palette and never reads prefers-color-scheme.
